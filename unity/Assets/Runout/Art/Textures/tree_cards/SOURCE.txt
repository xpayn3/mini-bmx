Tree / bush leaf cards, made by root tools/make_tree_cards.py (see its header for the command), downloaded 2026-11-26.
needle_branch.png  = ambientCG PineNeedles001 (CC0) needle bundles stamped along drawn twigs, at real scale
                     (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PineNeedles001)
leaf_cluster.png   = ambientCG LeafSet005 (CC0) leaves in a clump (bushes)   (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=LeafSet005)
leaf_tile.png      = the same leaves as a seamless patch (leafy crowns)
leaf_beech*.png    = Textures.com "3D Scanned European Beech Tree Leaves Atlas 01" (albedo, alpha, normal,
                     translucency). Textures.com licence: may be used inside the game, may NOT be redistributed as
                     files, so these are gitignored (the repo is public): run make_tree_cards.py with the atlas to make
                     them. Without them the game uses leaf_cluster / leaf_tile.
impostors.png/json = our own card trees rendered side-on by tools/bake_tree_impostors.tscn (billboards)
All: BC7 + mipmaps on import.
